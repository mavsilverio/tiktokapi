Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Clipper is a personal desktop application that runs only on its owner’s computer. It has no servers and does not send any data to the developer or to any third party other than the platforms the owner connects to (YouTube and TikTok).

What data is handled

  • Sign-in tokens. When the owner connects a YouTube or TikTok account, the access and refresh tokens issued by that platform are stored in files on the owner’s own computer. They are used only to make the requests described below.
  • Video content. Videos and clips from the owner’s own channel are downloaded and rendered temporarily on the owner’s computer and deleted afterwards.
  • Basic account identifiers. The TikTok user identifier returned at sign-in is kept in the local token file. No profile information is collected or displayed beyond that.

How the data is used

  • YouTube: reading the owner’s channel uploads and uploading clips to the owner’s channel.
  • TikTok (video.upload scope): sending clip files to the owner’s TikTok inbox as drafts and checking the status of those uploads.

Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Temporary video files are removed automatically. The owner can delete the stored tokens at any time (the Disconnect buttons in the app, or by deleting the token files) and can revoke Clipper’s access from the security settings of their Google and TikTok accounts.
