Clipper

Clipper is a small personal desktop application that turns long videos from my own YouTube channel into vertical short-form clips (1080x1920) and uploads them as Shorts.

What it does

  • Watches my own YouTube channel for newly published videos.
  • Finds the most active moments in each video and renders vertical clips.
  • Uploads the clips to YouTube and, optionally, sends them to the TikTok inbox of my own account as drafts, which I then review, caption and post myself in the TikTok app.

How TikTok is used

Clipper uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (the “upload to inbox” method, video.upload scope) only to send video files that I created to my own TikTok account. Nothing is posted publicly without me finishing the post inside the TikTok app.

Clipper runs entirely on my own computer. It is not a public service and has no users other than its owner.
